A major expansion is an expansion that adds new mechanics modifying the fundamental rules of the game.

Hunting Heroes

The major expansion "Hunting Heroes" adds a new category of card : heroes, with twelve cards added.

Obey the hero's strict law of the hall!

“Heroes are legendary figures among the Trueshot Lodge.”
A hero has a unique passive effect. These passive effects can modify the game by removing a card or changing a gamerule, for example. However, a hero’s effect takes precedence over any other effect during the game.


A rumor circulates that one of these heroes is seeking daring hunters to challenge a Legendary Beast roaming around the hall...


Before the third preparation phase of the game begins, the top card of the hero pile is revealed. The hero’s effect applies for the rest of the game.
